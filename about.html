What is Tenner?

Tenner is a social app for making and sharing top 10 lists — your favorite movies, restaurants, songs, books, vacation spots, anything. See how your picks compare with friends and groups, react and comment on each other's lists, and discover new things through the people you trust.

How it works

📝 Make a list

Pick a category — or create your own — and rank your top 10. Ranked or unranked, your call.

👥 Share with friends & groups

Send a list to specific friends, or post it to a group to see how everyone ranks the same things.

📊 Match scores & reveals

See how aligned your picks are with each friend — a suspenseful "tap to reveal" moment makes every comparison feel like a game.

🙈 Blind fill

Have your group fill out the same list without seeing each other's — then reveal together for a big moment.

💬 Comments & reactions

React with an emoji, drop a comment on a friend's list, or reply back — the fun's in the debate.

🎊 Weekly Tenner lists

A new prompt every week — "Top 10 Comfort Foods," "Top 10 Concerts," you name it. Build a streak by filling every week's list.

🌎 See all of Tenner

After each weekly reveals, compare your picks against the entire Tenner community — not just your circle.

🎁 Personalized gift pages

Every user gets a shareable gift page built from their Top 10 lists. Send the link to family — even people not on Tenner — and they can shop for you with zero guesswork.

🎁 Great for gift-giving

Never wonder what to get someone again. Every Tenner user has a personalized gift page you can browse — showing the movies they'd love, restaurants they'd eat at, sneakers they'd wear, and more, all pulled straight from their Top 10 lists.

Not on Tenner? You can still browse curated gift categories built from Tenner's community picks.

What Tenner is all about

The best relationships are built on shared interests, learning about (and from) each other, and a healthy dose of friendly trash talk about whose favorites are clearly superior. Tenner brings friends, family, and groups together to discover what you have in common, learn what makes each other tick, and spark the kind of conversations that make every relationship better. And along the way — by sharing and comparing lists with the people you know — you might just stumble onto your new favorite movie, TV show, restaurant, book, or you-fill-in-the-blank.

What data we collect

To make Tenner work, we collect your email and (if you sign in with Google) your name and profile picture. We store the lists you create, the friends and groups you connect with, the comments and reactions you post, and your email preferences. We may use this data to suggest gift ideas based on friends' lists, show app-wide aggregated insights (like the most-picked movie across all of Tenner), and personalize recommendations. You control which emails you receive from Tenner in your profile settings — and you can unsubscribe from any category at any time. We never sell your raw personal data. For full details, see Tenner's Privacy Policy and Tenner's Terms of Service. By using Tenner, you agree to both.
