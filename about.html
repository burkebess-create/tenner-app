About

What Tenner is about.

The short version is on the welcome page. This page is for the longer story — why it exists, what data it collects, and how to reach us.

The idea

The best relationships are built on shared interests, learning about (and from) each other, and a healthy dose of friendly trash talk about whose favorites are clearly superior.

Tenner brings friends, family, and groups together to discover what you have in common, learn what makes each other tick, and spark the kind of conversations that make every relationship better. And along the way — by sharing and comparing lists with the people you know — you might just stumble onto your new favorite movie, TV show, restaurant, book, or you-fill-in-the-blank.

A secondary but very real benefit: nobody in your circle should ever get a bad gift again. Every Tenner user has a personalized gift page built straight from their Top 10 lists — a shortcut for family, friends, and anyone else who wants to buy something they'll actually love.

Privacy & data

What we collect

To make Tenner work, we collect your email and (if you sign in with Google) your name and profile picture. We store the lists you create, the friends and groups you connect with, the comments and reactions you post, and your email preferences.

How we use it

We may use this data to suggest gift ideas based on your friends' lists, show app-wide aggregated insights (like the most-picked movie across all of Tenner), and personalize what we recommend. Your lists are only visible to your friends and group members — never to the wider community.

What we don't do

We never sell your raw personal data. You control which emails you receive from Tenner in your profile settings — and you can unsubscribe from any category at any time. For the full details, see Tenner's Privacy Policy and Tenner's Terms of Service. By using Tenner, you agree to both.

A note on affiliates

The Shop links on gift pages and gift-category pages are Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, Tenner earns a small commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. That's currently the only way Tenner makes money — no ads, no data sales.

Beta

Tenner is currently in beta. Things will get better, faster, and occasionally break. If something's off — or if you have an idea — hit us at contact@mytenner.com.
