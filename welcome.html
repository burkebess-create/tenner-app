How it works
Step 01

Make a list

Pick a category — or invent one — and drop in your Top 10. Ranked or unranked, your call. Movies, hikes, drinks, sneakers, anything.

Step 02

Add friends

Invite people with a link, or auto-connect when they sign up. Share lists one-on-one, in groups, or publicly to the whole community.

Step 03

See your match

A suspenseful tap-to-reveal shows how aligned your picks are — with a verdict from "Opposites attract" to "Twins separated at birth."

What's inside
🎁

Personal gift pages

Every user gets a shareable /g/ link — family shops from your actual favorites. No more guessing.

🎊

Weekly Tenner lists

A new community prompt every week. Build a streak. Compare with everyone.

💬

Comments & reactions

Drop a hot take on a friend's list. Debate their #1 pick. The fun is in the argument.

🙈

Hidden fills for groups

Everyone fills the same list without seeing each other's — then reveal together for a big moment.

📊

Match scores & jumps

See most-alike and most-different friends across every category. Discover new stuff through people you trust.

🌎

See all of Tenner

After each weekly reveals, compare against the entire community — not just your circle.

Great for gift-giving

Every Tenner user gets a personalized gift page — the movies, gear, books, and coffee they'd actually love, all pulled from their Top 10 lists. Send the link to family (even non-Tenner users) and they shop with zero guesswork.
