How it works
Step 01

Make a list

Pick a category — or create your own — and drop in your Top 10. Ranked or unranked, your call. Movies, TV Series, books, hikes, anything.

Step 02

Add friends

Search for friends already on Tenner, or invite new friends by sharing a link. Share lists one-on-one or in groups — only your friends and group members can see them.

Step 03

See your match scores

A suspenseful tap-to-reveal shows your match percentage and a verdict — from 32% "Opposites attract" to 91% "Twins separated at birth."

What's inside
🎁

Personal gift pages

Every user gets a shareable gift page built from their Top 10 lists. Browse your friends' gift pages to find the perfect gift based on things they already love, and send your own link to family and friends — even people not on Tenner. No more guessing.

🎊

Weekly Tenner lists

A new prompt every week — "Top 10 Comfort Foods," "Top 10 Concerts," you name it. Build a streak by filling every week's list.

💬

Comments & reactions

React with an emoji, drop a comment on a friend's list, or reply back — the fun's in the debate.

🙈

Hidden fills for groups

Everyone fills the same list without seeing each other's — then reveal together for a big moment.

📊

Match scores

See most-alike and most-different friends across every category. Discover new stuff through people you trust.

🌎

See all of Tenner

After each weekly reveals, compare against the entire community — not just your circle.
